This Privacy Policy describes how Style Factory 9 (business registration number 672-43-00632), operating under the brand name "AIKorea24" ("we", "us", "our"), collects, uses, and protects personal data in connection with AIKeep24 Pro (the "Service").

1.1 Free Tier

In the free tier, we collect no personal data. All AI conversation data is stored locally in your browser's IndexedDB and never transmitted to our servers.

  • Service delivery: Providing AI conversation storage, search, and sync functionality;

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Important: Free tier data is stored only in your browser. If you clear your browser data, it is permanently lost. We recommend regular backups via JSON/Markdown export.

4.1 Pro Cloud Data

  • While subscription is active: Retained indefinitely (so you can search and sync);
  • After subscription cancellation: Retained for 30 days, then permanently deleted;
  • Upon user request: Immediate deletion via info@aikorea24.kr.

4.2 Payment Information

Payment information is retained by Paddle according to their own policies. We do not have access to card details. See Paddle's Privacy Policy for details.

한국어 요약: Pro 구독 중에는 무기한 보관, 해지 후 30일 보관 후 영구 삭제됩니다. 사용자 요청 시 즉시 삭제 가능합니다.

5. Your Rights

Under applicable laws (PIPA, GDPR, CCPA), you have the following rights:

  • Right of access: Request a copy of personal data we hold about you;
  • Right to rectification: Request correction of inaccurate data;
  • Right to erasure (right to be forgotten): Request deletion of your personal data;
  • Right to data portability: Receive your data in a standard format (JSON);
  • Right to restrict processing: Request limitation of specific processing activities;
  • Right to object: Object to certain processing of your data.

To exercise these rights, please contact info@aikorea24.kr. We will respond within 30 days.

6. Security

We apply the following security measures:

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS 1.3);
  • Encryption at rest (Cloudflare D1 at-rest encryption);
  • License keys are hashed using bcrypt;
  • Principle of least privilege for access control;
  • Regular security audits and dependency updates.

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Cookies and Tracking

Our website (aikorea24.kr) uses only essential cookies. We do not use advertising tracking cookies or third-party analytics such as Google Analytics. Only Cloudflare's anonymous traffic analytics are used.

The browser extension does not use cookies.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed at children under 16. If we become aware that we have collected personal data from a child under 16 without verifiable parental consent, we will delete it immediately.

9. International Data Transfers

We use Cloudflare's global infrastructure, so your data may be processed outside Korea (e.g., in the EU or USA). All such transfers are conducted under appropriate legal safeguards, including GDPR Article 46 Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent protections.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may amend this Policy from time to time. For material changes, we will notify you by email or in-Service notice at least 30 days in advance.
