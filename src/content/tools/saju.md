Saju is an AI-powered Four Pillars (Bazi) chart service. Enter your birth date, time, and place, and it computes a real Four Pillars chart using exact solar terms, then explains what the chart says about work, money, and relationships — and what to do with it this week. The basic chart is free and shows the full calculation; the full reading adds seven in-depth sections, and a compatibility report compares two charts. It works in 17 languages on the web, with Google sign-in and no app install.

## How to Use

**1. Enter your birth details**

Fill in your date of birth, time of birth, gender, calendar (solar or lunar, including leap month), and birth city. If you don't know your birth time, the service computes six characters instead of eight and marks the hour pillar as estimated.

**2. Get your free chart**

Your four-pillar chart is calculated and saved immediately, with the calculation shown. You can reopen it anytime; charts made without signing in are deleted after 30 days.

**3. Read the free sections**

The free reading covers your chart's core pattern and what it means for work, money, and relationships, plus three things you can do before Friday.

**4. Go deeper when you want more**

A full reading ($9.99, 1 credit) unlocks seven in-depth sections. A compatibility report ($19.99, 2 credits) compares your chart with someone else's.
