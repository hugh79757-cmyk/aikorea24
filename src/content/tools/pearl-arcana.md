Pearl Arcana is an AI tarot reading service built around a 78-card deck drawn in mother-of-pearl (nacre) tones. You pick cards yourself from a face-down deck, and the AI interprets each card's meaning, its position in the spread, upright and reversed orientations, and how the cards relate to each other. Seven spreads are available: Mind (3 cards), Past–Present–Future (3), Love (3), Career (3), Yes/No (1), Horseshoe (7), and a one-card Daily reading. It works in 17 languages on the web with no app install, and you can sign in with an email magic link or Google.

## How to Use

**1. Ask your question and pick a spread**

Type what's on your mind and choose a spread that fits — a single card for a quick yes/no, three cards for love or career, or seven cards for a full Horseshoe reading.

**2. Pick your own cards**

Tap the face-down cards to choose them yourself. Each pick is revealed with its artwork before the reading begins.

**3. Read the AI interpretation**

The AI explains every card in the context of your question — its meaning, its position, whether it is upright or reversed, and how the cards combine into one story.

**4. Ask follow-ups and re-read the table**

Each reading allows up to 6 follow-up questions. Every follow-up can draw a clarifier card, and once you have two or more clarifiers you can ask the AI to re-read the whole table with everything on it.

## Pricing

- **Free**: a one-card daily reading plus a short free reading (about 500 characters). Logged-in users get up to 3 deep readings every 24 hours without spending credits, and new accounts start with 1 trial credit.

- **Credit packs** ($2.99 for 5 credits, $30.99 for 100 credits): each deep reading beyond the free allowance uses 1 credit.

**Refunds**: full refund within 7 days of purchase. Contact info@aikorea24.kr.

## Hands-on Test

We ran the full paid flow on 2026-11-14 and all four checks passed with no errors.

- **Sign-in and trial credit**: after magic-link sign-in, the account showed 1 trial credit and a 0/3 daily counter.
- **Deep yes/no reading**: asked whether to have dinner or not (Eight of Pentacles, reversed) — about 950 characters, ending with one thing to check before deciding.

- **Follow-ups and resynthesis**: two follow-up questions, each with a clarifier card picked directly, then a full-table re-read produced a four-paragraph synthesis.

## Pros and Cons

**Pros**

- You pick the cards yourself instead of having them assigned, which makes each reading feel personal.
- Deep readings interpret positions, reversals, and card combinations together rather than listing generic meanings.
- Follow-up questions with clarifier cards and a full-table re-read let you dig into one question instead of starting over.
- 17 languages, free daily reading, and no app install — it runs entirely in the browser.

**Cons**

- AI readings are for reflection and entertainment, not guaranteed predictions about the future.
- The Horseshoe spread and deep readings beyond the free allowance require Premium or credits.
- There is no mobile app; everything runs in the browser.
