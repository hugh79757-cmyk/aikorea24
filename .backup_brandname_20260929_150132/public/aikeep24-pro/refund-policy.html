1. Refund Eligibility

1.1 14-Day Money-Back Guarantee

If you request a refund within 14 days of your initial purchase, we will refund you in full for any reason. This applies to all of the following:

  • Monthly subscriptions
  • Annual subscriptions
  • Lifetime licenses (limited launch edition)

1.2 After 14 Days

After 14 days, refunds are available only in the following cases:

  • Duplicate charges: Full refund if you were charged twice for the same subscription;
  • Service outage: Pro-rata refund if the Service is unavailable for 30+ days due to our fault;
  • Technical defect: Refund if core functionality does not work despite our reasonable efforts.

1.3 Non-Refundable Cases

Refunds are not available in the following cases:

  • License cancellation due to violation of our Terms of Service;
  • Detected sharing, resale, or fraudulent use of license keys;
  • Auto-renewal charges requested for refund after 14 days (except as provided in Section 1.2);
  • Temporary compatibility issues caused by third-party AI platform (ChatGPT, Claude, Genspark, etc.) policy or UI changes (we will address these within a reasonable timeframe).
한국어 요약: 약관 위반, 라이선스 도용, 제3자 플랫폼의 일시적 UI 변경 등은 환불 대상에서 제외됩니다.

2. How to Request a Refund

2.1 Paddle Customer Portal (Recommended)

The fastest way is to request a refund directly through the Paddle customer portal, accessible via the link in your purchase confirmation email. Paddle is our Merchant of Record and processes refund requests automatically.

  1. Open the Paddle receipt email from your purchase;
  2. Click "Manage subscription";
  3. Select "Request refund";
  4. Provide a reason (optional);
  5. Submit.

2.2 Direct Email Request

If you cannot access the Paddle portal, email info@aikorea24.kr with the following information:

  • Email address used at purchase;
  • Paddle order number (from receipt email);
  • Reason for refund (optional, helps us improve);
  • Purchase date.

3. Processing Time

  • Approval: Refund requests are reviewed and approved within 1-3 business days;
  • Disbursement: Approved refunds are processed within 5-10 business days to the original payment method;
  • Card refunds: May take additional time to appear on your statement depending on your card issuer's policies (typically 1-2 billing cycles).
한국어 요약: 환불 요청 접수 후 1-3 영업일 이내 검토 및 승인, 5-10 영업일 이내에 원 결제 수단으로 송금됩니다.

4. Data Handling After Refund

Once a refund is completed, the following occurs:

  • Your license key is immediately invalidated;
  • Cloud sync is suspended;
  • AI conversation data stored in the cloud is retained for 30 days after the refund (recoverable if you repurchase);
  • After 30 days, cloud data is permanently deleted;
  • Data stored locally (in IndexedDB) is unaffected and remains on your device.

If you want your cloud data deleted immediately after a refund, please request it at info@aikorea24.kr.

5. Cancellation vs. Refund

These are different concepts:

  • Cancellation: Stops your next renewal but lets you keep access until the end of your current billing period. No refund is issued. Available anytime via the Paddle portal.
  • Refund: Returns your payment and ends Service access immediately. Subject to the 14-day money-back guarantee or Section 1.2.
한국어 요약: "구독 해지"는 다음 갱신만 중단하고 현재 기간까지는 이용 가능합니다. "환불"은 즉시 종료 + 결제 금액 반환입니다.

6. Dispute Resolution

If you are dissatisfied with how your refund request was handled, we recommend the following escalation path:

  1. First: Reply to info@aikorea24.kr requesting reconsideration;
  2. Second: Contact Paddle's customer support directly at paddle.com/support/buyers;
  3. Third: For Republic of Korea residents, file a dispute with the Korea Consumer Agency (www.kca.go.kr, dial 1372).

We prioritize customer satisfaction and aim to resolve all reasonable refund requests promptly.
