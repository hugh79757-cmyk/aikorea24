<objective>
Eliminate the Threads dual-scheduling race condition by removing the `--daemon` mode from `main_v3.py`. Launchd becomes the single scheduling mechanism — no internal `schedule` loop.

Purpose: Fix THR-01 — when both launchd fires the script AND the internal `schedule.every(2).hours.do(run_v3)` loop runs, two pipeline executions can overlap, causing duplicate posts and resource contention. The fix is minimal: remove `--daemon`, keep only `--once` and `--dry-run`.

Output: `scripts/threads/main_v3.py` (modified — --daemon removed)
</objective>
