- **Fixed**: `cumulative_offset - trans_dur` (클립 종료 시점 - 전환 시간)로 모두 수정
- **Affected functions**: `build_scene_filter_chain`, `build_render_command`, `render_carousel_video`, `render_reel_video`
- **Result**: v0 마지막 프레임 0.5초 freeze 현상 제거

- TEXT_HEAVY_TYPES (HOOK/CONFLICT/TWIST/EXPANSION) = `wipeleft`/`slideleft`/`circleopen`

- Applied in `render_reel_video` and `render_carousel_video` (when scenes provided)
